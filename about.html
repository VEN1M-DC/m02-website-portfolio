I’m studying the foundations of web development: how a page is structured, how an application is organized, and how people move through an interface.

A practical approach

This portfolio brings those topics together in a small, readable site. Its three pages share navigation and use the same spacing, colors, and content structure.

01 / Build with purpose

Give each page a clear role. Use headings and HTML landmarks to organize the content.

02 / Test the experience

Check the layout on narrow screens, follow links, and move through the page with a keyboard.

03 / Revise and record

Address problems, document the decisions, and save meaningful changes with Git.
